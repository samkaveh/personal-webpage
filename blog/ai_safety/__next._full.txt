10:T72b,Probably you have heard that language models are trained on trying to predict the next word in a sequence where these sequences are all the data on the internet. This is to some extent true but does leave out a lot of details that are important for investigating the claim on eradicating humanity. Language models like most other deep learning methods rely on optimising a target objective during their training phase. This means they will automatically pick up patterns from input data and store that in their parameters by optimising what the architects of these models set as target. If you think about optimising the target of predicting the next word (token) in a random sentence from the internet you already see that this does not seem to explain the intelligence these models show in practice. Indeed this is only the first phase of training a language model, where a lot of curated and not curated data is presented to the model to let it pick up language patterns and store knowledge as much as possible. The LLMs at this phase are not very helpful and usable since the target they are trained on is good for generating text which incorporates knowledge but does not mean this will be useful to the user. The output also lacks control for correctness as any knowledge stored in the model at this phase is considered as correct as any other knowledge. This phase of training LLMs is often called the pre-training phase. The initial release of chatbots that only had this type of training resulted in disappointments, negative publicity for some large companies due to inappropriate outputs generated by these chatbots that even in some cases made the companies pull the plug on development or decelerate the efforts. 1a:T530,The researchers working on these problems came up with multiple new methods to resolve these issues, and if you have interacted with LLMs recently you see that they get better and better in tool usage and understanding your intents. The breakthrough came as researchers used methods previously used to teach deep learning models how to play computer games, chess or the game of Go. They used an already pre-trained LLM and presented it with previously curated instructions, the needed inputs and wished outputs and let it optimise on matching the outputs. These instructions also included following patterns in tool usage and resulted in models being able to effectively use tools. They also extended these instructions to be multi-turn (as in a conversation between two people working on the same set of slides discussing the best approach to get to the wished result) or instructions on doing some task in multiple steps that resulted in what we call today as reasoning models or thinking in LLMs. Reasoning models in principle play the turns of instructions with themselves instead of an outside user for a few turns and present the final result to the user. These instructions are not necessarily human-generated, as the current LLMs running in sandboxes can generate many of these for different special use cases or topics.13:["$","p","p-3",{"children":"$1a"}]
