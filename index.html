01A brief noteEngineering · Intelligence · Systems

Engineering systems with rigor and precision.

I am a Staff Software Engineer focused on applied ML/AI and agentic systems at Blue Yonder, where I architect and operate customer-facing AI services. I lead the design of intelligent agent harnesses for demand forecasting and MLOps, event-driven query engines aggregating over billions of rows, and developer interfaces using agent-to-agent protocols.

With a PhD in Physics from the University of Hamburg and scientific ML research at CERN and DESY, my background combines mathematical problem-solving with the practical realities of shipping software. As a former startup founder, I value end-to-end ownership: from technical planning with stakeholders through to deployment, monitoring, and production reliability.

I develop primarily in Python, Rust, and C++, speak fluent English and German, and am happiest turning ambiguous, high-stakes technical challenges into simple, deterministic systems.

— Sam Kaveh, PhD
